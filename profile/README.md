## Who we are

Harrison Ward Technology is an IT services firm. We act as the technology partner for organizations that need enterprise-grade IT without the enterprise-grade headcount. Some clients hand us the whole stack. Others keep an internal team and bring us in to cover the gaps.

## What we do

| Service | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Managed IT** | Full-stack support, monitoring, patching, and help desk |
| **Co-managed IT** | We extend your existing internal team instead of replacing it |
| **vCIO / vCTO** | Strategic technology leadership, roadmapping, and budget planning |
| **Security & Compliance** | Hardening, policy, audit readiness, and incident response |
| **Procurement** | Hardware and software sourcing, licensing, and lifecycle management |
| **3D Printing** | On-demand prototyping and production printing |

## What lives here

This organization holds the code we build to run our practice and support our clients:

- Automation scripts and RMM tooling
- Internal tools and dashboards
- Infrastructure as code and deployment templates
- Client-facing utilities and integrations
- Documentation and runbooks

Public repos are open for anyone to use. Private repos are client work and stay that way.
